# Audio credits

`menu_surf.wav` and `menu_gulls.wav`: original synthetic shore wash and distant
seagull calls generated by `tools/audio/generate_menu_beach.py` in this repository.
No recordings, samples, downloads, paid tools or accounts are used. Rebuild with
`python tools/audio/generate_menu_beach.py` (Python standard library).

To the extent possible under law, the authors waive all copyright and related
or neighboring rights in these assets. Distributed without warranty.

Both files are 32-second, 22,050 Hz stereo PCM loops. Surf uses a one-second
baked overlap crossfade; gulls are silent at the loop boundary. Godot loops the
samples directly, without a timer or a finished-signal restart.

In-game layers `game_wash.wav`, `game_wind.wav`, `game_harbour.wav`,
`game_ice.wav`, `game_rain.wav` and `game_desert.wav`: original procedural
audio from `tools/audio/generate_game_ambience.py` (uses the PCM writer in
`generate_menu_beach.py`). Source and generated assets are dedicated to CC0
1.0 Universal under the same waiver above. No external recordings or samples.
Rebuild: `python tools/audio/generate_game_ambience.py`. All are 32-second,
22,050 Hz stereo PCM loops with a one-second overlap seam. Harbour combines
crane motors, metal squeaks and horns; ice combines wind and creaks; rain
combines rainfall and city hum. In-game sea/tropical surf and tropical gulls
reuse the credited menu loops above.

Music (Patch 3.1), in `assets/audio/music/`: `day_Neon_Buoy.mp3` ("Neon Buoy"),
`sunset_Sunlit_Current.mp3` ("Sunlit Current") and
`night_The_Blackwater_Passage.mp3` ("The Blackwater Passage"). Credit:
Andy Viera (original). Played by `game_music.gd`, one song per time of day.

Plane engines (Patch 4.0.5), made in code by
`features/planes/plane_engine_sound.gd` at run time; no files, recordings,
samples or downloads. Bush prop (piston), Regional turboprop, Cargo jet
(turbofan) and Fighter jet (turbine and afterburner) are each one-second
22,050 Hz loops built from whole-cycle tones and filtered noise. The code and
the sound it makes are dedicated to CC0 1.0 Universal under the same waiver
above.
